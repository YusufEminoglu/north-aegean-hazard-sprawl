THE OBSERVED TRAJECTORY

Four decades of growth set the stage

The urban footprint expanded from 5,814.81 ha to 23,988.24 ha, concentrating development in the Gediz and Bakırçay alluvial plains. Those same accessible lowlands carry overlapping flood and seismic constraints.

The model asks a planning question: if the historical spatial logic continues, where does the next 9,866.7 ha of urban land go—and what changes when ecological penalties shape allocation?
